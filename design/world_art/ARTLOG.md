# World art log (Environment / Level Artist)

Scope: build the world the lore describes. Red Reaches and Terrarium One first, then a second region proof (Lumen Depths).
Engine budget: Godot 4.7 Compatibility, phone / Switch-class. Draw calls <= 150 (full flow), primitives <= 220k (was 328k).
Screens come from `tools/shot.sh` (software GL under Xvfb, 1280x720). All paths below are in `/tmp/claude-0/` unless noted;
the images that matter are copied into `design/world_art/shots/` at the end.

## 0. How the kit is built (so the next artist can extend it)

* `tools/modeling/world/` (bpy as a module, headless): `kitlib.py` (mesh builder, per-vertex colour paint, BVH-ray AO bake, atlas UVs,
  GLB export), `pieces_rocks.py` (strata mesas / buttes / hoodoos / arch / boulders / scree / spoil / salt crust),
  `pieces_struct.py` (Spanwright span bay + pier + gate tower, marker stone, cairns, load posts, well head, awning, vault door,
  Thoughtstone, Dominion derrick / collar / pylon / flags / stacks, glyph wall, Red Span stump ...), `pieces_flora.py`,
  `pieces_hub.py` (Scale Transition Array gate, ducts with spore growth, planters, tanks, lamp strings ...), `pieces_lumen.py`
  (crystal spires, glow colonies, lantern kelp, glass bloom, bell-moss, basalt columns, hanging walkway, den).
  Run `python3 tools/modeling/world/build_reaches_kit.py [piece ...]` then `godot --headless --import`.
* Output: one GLB per piece in `game/art/world/kit/` (<= 2k tris each, most 100-1.5k), COLOR_0 = hand-painted colour x baked AO,
  UV0 = shared 512 px decal atlas (`kit_atlas.webp`: glyph band, tally scratches, hazard stripes, Dominion stencil, wood, rope, salt,
  rust, cracks, pulse-lock, ashlar joints, lichen, planks) -- only decal faces carry real UVs, everything else points at the blank cell.
* `kit_prop.gdshader` = `toon_prop` + atlas decals + wind sway (foliage) + bluish-atlas-pixels-glow. `glow_pulse.gdshader` = the
  emissive surface (per-cell phase pulse). Material slots in the GLB: `kit` and `glow`.
* `ReachesKit` (loader / material factory), `ReachesLandmarks` (JSON-driven placement; every group merged into one mesh per
  material on the WorkerThreadPool, attached one group per frame), `ReachesAtmosphere` (light shafts + chasm mist).
  Placements are generated by `tools/gen_world_art.py` (-> `game/world/red_reaches/world_art.json`) and `tools/gen_lumen_art.py`.

## 1. Baseline (before this pass) -- review of `b1_*.png`, `base_*.png` (v0.13.0)

Cameras: `tools/qa/scripts/art_vistas_a.json` through `Terrain.qa_vista` (free camera, HUD hidden). Numbers: full flow peak
**147 draw calls / 327,208 primitives** (`ff0/log.txt`).

Candid critique against the lore and the region mood sheet:

1. **The lore's scale is not there.** Nothing in the level is bigger than ~12 m. The Gate is a 3.5 m ring, the "Ochre Span" is a
   plank-and-rope-looking deck with box piers, the Augur site is a 9 m lattice next to grey boxes, the boss rig is a grey brick.
   There is no landmark readable from far, so wayfinding is only the UI arrow.
2. **Terrain strata are good, the horizon is empty.** The tiered mesa walls with painted strata read well close up, but every
   backdrop mesa is the same noisy heightfield blob; no buttes, hoodoos, arches, caprock overhangs.
3. **No history in the dirt.** Lore says "generations maintain bridges, wells and roads", Spanwright notices, tally scratches, load
   limits on the rail, cairns of names, Dominion spoil heaps and survey flags. Agent 2's props are small procedural boxes; none of the
   big stories (Red Span remnant, extraction scars) is legible at a glance.
4. **Flora/ecology flat.** "Flat tree" is a dead-stick, lichen is a rock with a cap, no terraces for the grazer herd to keep open,
   no boneyards. Everything is one red.
5. **Atmosphere is a single fog.** No light shafts, no layered haze in the chasms, nothing that makes a 40 m drop feel deep.
6. **Budget.** 327k primitives; the sun shadow pass alone is ~half (terrain redrawn at full resolution into both cascades).

Fix plan: author a real kit (strata mesas, Spanwright stone bays/piers/towers, marker/cairn/well/vault, Dominion derrick/collar/
flags/stacks, flora, salt, thoughtstone), place it as landmarks + skyline + dressing, rebuild the Ochre Span from the kit,
add shafts/mist, add terrain LOD + a cheap shadow proxy, merge scatter.
