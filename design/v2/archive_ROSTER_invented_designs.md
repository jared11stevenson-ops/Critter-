# CRITTER v2 — The Seven (original designs, Lead, 2026-10-11)

Placeholder designs made by the Lead; the creator will designate changes. Each critter is a real-insect-inspired *person* with a weapon, a fighting rhythm,
a silhouette that reads at 64 px, and a seven-chapter Life Story unlocked only by staying alive. Power is balanced by a **36-point budget** (shapes differ, totals equal),
verified by the CI bot tournament (GDD 5.5).

Stat scale 1-10: **Vit**ality, **Sta**mina, **Poi**se, **Spd** (move + action speed), **Rch** (reach), **Dmg**. Sum = 36 for everyone.

## 1. Haldor — "The Quarry-Hammer"
- **Look:** barrel-chested charcoal rhino beetle with a single swept horn capped in gold; a hard hat dented to a crescent; one cracked eye-spot; a tiny chisel on a cord.
  Silhouette: wide shoulders, horn forward, hammer as a huge square mass. Face: sleepy half-lidded dot eyes, small mouth.
- **Weapon:** Quarry Maul: a shed shell chunk bound to a haft. Long wind-ups, huge arcs.
- **Strike:** slow arcs (startup 16f light, 36f heavy); **Guard:** wide shield-block with the maul; **Grab:** two-hand shoulder-barge throw.
- **Abilities:** *Quake Slam* (ground cone, knockdown) / *Horn Charge* (super-armor charge; ring-out). **Last Stand: Landslide** (three escalating slams).
- **Personality:** gentle giant, mumbles, loves rocks, hates fighting but is great at it.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Dust Lung*: sealed, grumpy and coughing. (2) *Quarry Day*: how he breaks giant statues for a living. (3) *The Chisel*: he secretly carves tiny faces. (4) *Cave-In*: a near-death memory of saving a crew. (5) *The Foreman's Debt*: why he has to fight. (6) *Stone vs. Antler*: his rivalry with Kragg. (7) *The Last Statue*: he finally carves the thing he always wanted. **Trial:** win a bout without using Quake Slam.

## 2. Sessa — "The Gentle Edge"
- **Look:** tall, thin chartreuse mantis; black blade forearms with magenta edge-glow; oversized glossy compound eyes; a tiny scarf. Silhouette: V-shaped blades, thin waist.
  Face: wide manic cheerful eyes, a calm smile.
- **Weapon:** Twin Reapers.
- **Strike:** fastest in the game (10f lights, 3-hit chains, low damage per hit but high total); **Guard:** crossed blades with the longest parry window (10f) but lowest Poise; **Grab:** blade-lock throw.
- **Abilities:** *Riposte Stance* (any strike is auto-parried into a combo) / *Blur Step* (behind the opponent). **Last Stand: Thousand Cuts.**
- **Personality:** cheerful, polite, scary-calm, treats every duel like a tea ceremony.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Polite Introductions* (2) *The Academy* (mantis school where the loser is... eaten) (3) *First Refusal* (she refused the rule) (4) *Near Silence* (near-death: she hears nothing) (5) *The Dragonfly Question* (6) *Zell*: her rival (7) *The Gentle Edge*: she redefines the school. **Trial:** win three bouts in a row without taking damage.

## 3. Weft — "The Thread Between"
- **Look:** violet orb-weaver, big round abdomen with silver web pattern; eight limbs (four graceful, four tool-like); a lantern of teal glow on her back; thick glasses with spiral lenses.
  Silhouette: round body, long thin limbs spread like a star. Face: worried eyes, sheepish smile.
- **Weapon:** a long thread-lance (needle) plus silk lines (whip/tether).
- **Strike:** longest reach (9), medium speed; **Guard:** silk bundle (weak, long parry window); **Grab:** *Tether Pull* (ranged grab that drags the enemy in).
- **Abilities:** *Thread Snare* (root/tether) / *Web Mine* (ground trap). **Last Stand: Silk Cathedral** (arena web slows the enemy, speeds her).
- **Personality:** shy, anxious, brilliant; weaves everyone she meets into a tapestry; nervous jokes.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Knots* (2) *The Sister Webs* (3) *Tapestry of Strangers* (4) *The Flood* (near-death: she cut her own web to save someone) (5) *The Hungry Neighbors* (6) *Marro*: her rival (7) *The Last Thread*. **Trial:** win a bout using only abilities and silk (no direct strikes).

## 4. Cinder — "Pressure Cooker"
- **Look:** squat tangerine bombardier beetle with soot-black armor plates and yellow pressure gauges; a steel-and-brass gauntlet with a nozzle; goggles pushed up on his head; always slightly smoking.
  Silhouette: round and stubby with a long nozzle arm. Face: wide eyes, jittery grin, one eyebrow always raised.
- **Weapon:** Boiler Gauntlet.
- **Strike:** mid reach nozzle jabs; builds **Pressure** (resource) on every hit; **Guard:** hiss-vent (reflects a small blast on parry); **Grab:** nozzle-hook pull.
- **Abilities:** *Pressure Burst* (spend Pressure for a cone blast, bigger with more) / *Scorch Spray* (damage-over-time zone). **Last Stand: Overpressure** (massive blast; costs 30% current Vitality, cannot kill himself).
- **Personality:** loud, funny, insecure; explodes when anxious; dreams of being a firework-maker.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Pop* (2) *The Workshop* (3) *Wet Fuses* (4) *The Big One* (near-death: an explosion he caused) (5) *The Apology Tour* (6) *The Toad Debt* (rival species: Mudge toads) (7) *Grand Finale*. **Trial:** win with Pressure at maximum when the bout ends.

## 5. Marro — "We Are One"
- **Look:** brick-red army ant, tall rectangular brass shield (a flattened coin), a straight pike of bone; a tiny helmet with a plume of her colony's thread; her body is a stack of three segments with a visible stripe pattern.
  Silhouette: shield rectangle plus pike line. Face: small, serious dot eyes, tiny frown; occasionally a huge smile.
- **Weapon:** Bulwark & Pike.
- **Strike:** pike thrusts (good reach, low damage); **Guard:** shield (best in game; strong chip resistance); **Grab:** shield bash (stagger more than damage).
- **Abilities:** *Shield Wall* (invulnerable frontal stance, drains stamina) / *Pike Lunge* (long guard-crush thrust). **Last Stand: Colony Call** (spectral ant-swarm assists for 5 s).
- **Personality:** loyal, literal, stoic; speaks in "we"; learning what "I" means alone.
- **Life Story:** (1) *The Count* (she was one of ten thousand) (2) *Colony Song* (3) *The Quiet* (the colony's voices are gone in the jar) (4) *Lost in the Swarm* (near-death memory) (5) *First "I"* (6) *Weft*: her rival (7) *We Are One*. **Trial:** survive five Blood Bouts using only Guard-based finishes.

## 6. Zell — "Short Summer"
- **Look:** sleek cyan dragonfly with four transparent glass wings, white racing stripes, a long slim glaive with a wing-shaped blade; huge goggle eyes; a scarf tail.
  Silhouette: wing X shape, long thin weapon. Face: smirk, one eye bigger than the other.
- **Weapon:** Glass Glaive.
- **Strike:** quick reach hits with aerial variations; **Guard:** minimal (a feather guard), relies on **Air Dodge** (extra dodge in air); **Grab:** dive-snatch.
- **Abilities:** *Skybound* (leap, then dive-strike) / *Glass Gale* (ranged wing slash). **Last Stand: Final Flight** (invulnerable flurry of aerial passes).
- **Personality:** cocky speedster, funny, charming, and he knows his species lives short, so he lives loud.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Nymph* (2) *First Flight* (3) *Short Summer* (he knows) (4) *Dive* (near-death memory) (5) *The Race* (6) *Sessa*: his rival (7) *Long Summer* (he chooses to live longer than he should). **Trial:** win a bout without touching the ground.

## 7. Kragg — "Main Event"
- **Look:** massive mahogany stag beetle, huge bone-white mandibles, moss patch on his back, cape of tattered banner, a heavy chain around his waist with a hook. Showman's grin.
  Silhouette: huge pincers wide, cape. Face: giant confident grin, one eyebrow ring.
- **Weapon:** Pincer Chain (mandibles plus chain hook).
- **Strike:** short reach, hard hits; **Guard:** mandible clamp (blocks and traps); **Grab:** best in game (wide, unblockable *if* it hits; ring-out).
- **Abilities:** *Pincer Hold* (command grab) / *Chain Pull* (hook that drags enemy in). **Last Stand: Main Event** (a cinematic throw with a huge damage multiplier).
- **Personality:** showman, trash-talking, secretly kind; treats every fight as a performance.
- **Life Story:** (1) *Introductions* (2) *The Circuit* (underground wrestling league) (3) *Heel Turn* (4) *The Pit* (near-death) (5) *Real Name* (6) *Haldor*: his rival (7) *Main Event*: the match that matters. **Trial:** win a bout using only grabs and ring-outs.

## Production notes for artists and animators
- **Build rule:** every critter is a hierarchy of rigid parts (head, thorax, abdomen, upper/lower limbs, wings, weapon). Insects are naturally segmented, so no skinning is needed.
- **Animation:** procedural, from per-move pose keys (anticipation, strike, recovery) plus squash and stretch, impact frames, smear shapes. See ART_BIBLE.md.
- **Readability tests:** each critter must be identifiable (a) as a flat black silhouette at 64 px and (b) by its color accent at 32 px.
- **Faces:** simple, dot-based, strongly expressive (6 expressions: calm, grin, angry, hurt, scared, dying).
