# Counterplay and skill stars (creator ruling, supersedes the "no counters" reading)

**Rule.** Counters are allowed and wanted, but they must be *answers a skilled player performs*, never hidden stat multipliers. Every style has things it answers well and things it has to work around; both sides get equal chances to answer. A status-heavy critter is rewarded for using its kit; a critter without a dedicated answer must never be an automatic loss. Some critters are stronger than others only in certain aspects; overall every critter is equally strong (target ~50% at equal skill).

**Skill stars (1-3).** How much thinking a kit asks of the player. Shown on the select screen, shelf, jar and scouting card. Stars are never a strength rating: a 3-star critter is not stronger or weaker than a 1-star one, it asks more of you.
| Stars | Critters | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aruun | One direct kit: mace pressure, Gravity Pull, Rage that builds as you fight |
| 2 | Zephyr, Nyxaris, Pharilux, Scarlith | A resource, zone or status to manage alongside the fight |
| 3 | Bramvex, Mollusk, Cigarra | Plans and timing: Intel and terrain, borrowed time and debt, premonition and compulsion |

## Universal answers (every critter has these, all skill-based)
- **Guard** blocks strikes; **Parry** (timed guard) stuns the attacker, refunds stamina and now also **cleanses every status, damage-over-time and compulsion** on the parrying critter. This is everyone's answer to status builds and mind control.
- **Dodge** i-frames beat strikes, grabs and projectiles; **Grab (Guard+Strike)** beats Guard; **Last Stand** breaks stun.
- **Tenacity:** the same control effect (slow, root, confuse) applied again inside 4 seconds lands at half length. The first application is rewarded in full; chain-locking is impossible. Zone refreshes are exempt (a zone is avoided by moving, which is skill).

## Gaps to close (each critter should have an answer to each *kind* of threat)
Threat kinds: status/control, zones/projectiles, grabs, mobility/escape, burst. A kit-by-kit audit and any missing answer (for example a status-cleanse or immunity moment for Aruun, an anti-zone answer for Mollusk) is the next balance task; every new answer must be a skill action, with a cost, not a passive number.
