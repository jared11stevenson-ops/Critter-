# CRITTER v2 — The Seven (LORE CHARACTERS; Lead, 2026-10-11, replaces the invented roster)

**Correction:** the first v2 roster used invented characters. That was a misreading. The roster is now drawn from the Master Lore Bible's First Ten. The creator's
approved designs are **binding**: silhouette, anatomy, costume, equipment and palette come from the reference art. The only thing that changes is the *rendering style*
(the cute-grim ink-and-gold look in ART_BIBLE.md), applied to the approved designs without altering what makes each character recognizable. Backstories come from the
lore (Book VI and XIV) and are delivered as the survival-unlocked Life Story chapters. The rest of the lore stays minimal in play.

**Reference art (read these before building anything):** `tools/source_art/*_sheet.*` (original sheets), `design/reference_packs/<id>/` (ortho cuts, expressions, detail), `design/v2/lore_text/LORE_BIBLE_v12.txt`
(text; each character's section). Per-character source sheets are listed in `design/reference_packs/INDEX.md`.

Rival-species chapter (6) for each: use a lore-appropriate rival or antagonist (Helix/Dominion enforcers, regional predators), decided with the creator.

## Art rules for the lore cast
1. **Binding designs:** match silhouette, anatomy, costume, equipment, palette from the reference sheets. Do not humanize. Do not simplify away a defining feature.
2. **Style:** cute-grim ink-and-gold rendering (cel shading, outlines, expressive faces, chunky readable proportions in the ART_BIBLE sense). "Chunky" means readable at 64 px, never a different design.
3. **Review loop:** each critter gets a side-by-side board (reference vs in-engine) for the creator; the creator's eye is final.
4. **Existing rig system stays:** CritterRig, toon and outline shaders, pose library, faces and FX are reusable; only the seven character builds change.
