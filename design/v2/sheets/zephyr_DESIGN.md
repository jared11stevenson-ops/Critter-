**Species language:** hawk-moth: fuzzy round thorax, banded abdomen with tail tuft, four wings (olive forewings with orange underwing), long proboscis, feathered antennae.

**Palette:** olive-brown fuzz, orange underwing, nectar-magenta accent, pollen-gold glow.
**Rig:** 9 parts; `hover` lift + fast wing flap on the glass `extra` part; satchel lag part.
**Still short:** the proboscis scarf reads as stacked rings; antenna flags look like bunny ears from the front; no wing-blur shapes yet; face is a plate (eyes read, mouth tiny); wand is a plain stick.
