# Bramvex - design note (pass 4, from REDESIGN_BRIEFS)
**Lore anchors:** prickly stick insect; ragged membranous wings; field equipment; restrained red insignia; huge thorned weapon "Diplomacy"; a tired, careful sergeant (no hat/uniform bulk; the sergeant reads from the harness and posture).
**Species language:** stick insect: three slender thorax segments, very long jointed bark limbs with knobby joints and thorn ridges, long thorned antennae, narrow head with mandibles.
**Changed vs 2D sheet:** dropped the peaked cap and jacket (not insect-true), made limbs far longer/thinner, Diplomacy is now taller than he is, wings are translucent glass folded as a cloak, the red is limited to one armband.
**Palette:** bark brown / olive / dusty tan + red accent + amber lantern glow.
**Rig:** 9 rigid parts, two-handed club (`arm_b_follow`), wings flap/sway part, lantern + sash lag part.
**Still short:** folded wings are small and read as a bundle, not a cloak; the face is a small plate; harness pouches are simple blobs; red armband is hard to see at distance; Diplomacy's thorns are uniform.
