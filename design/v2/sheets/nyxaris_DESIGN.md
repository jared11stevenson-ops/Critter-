# Nyxaris - design note (pass 4, from REDESIGN_BRIEFS)
**Lore anchors:** cryogenic silk and venom; "fully nonhuman"; silk as art, bridges, traps, records; patient until a boundary is crossed; the Cold Archive.
**Species language:** orb-weaver spider: upright cephalothorax, six long jointed legs (3 in each leg group, both sides) + two slender silk-wand arms, big abdomen, spinnerets, chelicerae with venom fangs, furred face with small eyes.
**Changed vs 2D sheet:** the crab/yeti claw build became a tall elegant spider; the crystal fan became an ice-flower pattern of petals on the abdomen; added the translucent silk train from the frost-crystal spinnerets; fangs frame the face; pink teardrop pendants kept as accent.
**Palette:** deep indigo, ice-blue glow, white-silver silk, pink-magenta flower accents.
**Rig:** 9 parts; silk train is a glass `extra` part (lags); wand on the front arm for silk lines.
**Still short:** abdomen petals are small and read as spikes, not a flower; the silk train is a bundle of straight ribbons; legs are thin and uniform; the face plate is cute but not "eerie elegant"; no boundary-web or cocoon FX yet.
