# Scarlith - design note (pass 4, from REDESIGN_BRIEFS)
**Lore anchors:** red-velvet-mite design, cheese/pirate flavor; broker; everything has value.
**Species language:** velvet mite: round plush idiosoma, eight short legs (4 per leg group), fuzzy pile suggested by tufts, mouthparts.
**Changed vs 2D sheet:** the cheese carapace slab on the back became a barrel + bottle rack with a rind lid (clearer, modelable); coat simplified to wine panels with gold buttons and rind-trim tatters; added eyepatch, gold tooth, coin purse; 8 legs instead of 4.
**Palette:** crimson velvet / cheese-gold / wine purple / rind orange + warm gold glow.
**Rig:** 9 parts; boarding-hook polearm on the front arm; lantern lag part; barrel/bottle part sways.
**Still short:** the plush fuzz is just sparse spikes (no soft layer), body reads flat red; hat corners oversized; lantern reads as a white tag; no cheese-hole motif on the barrel lid.
