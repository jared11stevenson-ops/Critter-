# Mollusk - design note (pass 4, from REDESIGN_BRIEFS)
**Lore anchors:** giant African snail; layered shell carved with journey marks; love of forklifts; gentle, patient, rescue hero.
**Species language:** snail: heavy soft body, long muscular foot slab + tapering tail, two long eye-stalks, spiral banded shell.
**Changed vs 2D sheet:** dropped the jungle plants, charms and stone mace; added the high-vis orange rescue vest + reflective stripes + harness, rescue-crew patches on the shell, carved tick marks + spiral apex, a small forklift-fork prop and tags on the harness; face is tired-lidded stalk eyes with a downturned mouth.
**Palette:** shell brown/amber bands, olive-grey body, high-vis orange accent, amber glow.
**Rig:** 9 parts; the shell is one `extra` part (sway only); no weapon part, strikes are body/fist.
**Still short:** the body is more upright-humanoid than a low snail; the foot/tail trail is a flat wedge; shell bands are plain rings (no carved glyph variety); stalk eyes do not emote beyond lids; legs are stumps.
