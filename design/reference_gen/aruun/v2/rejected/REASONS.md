# v2 rejects (kept for the record; nothing here is approved)
1. `front_proxy_silhouette_v0.png` - front body whose silhouette is the proxy's own front raster. REJECTED: the proxy is a depth-light tube fit to side+back, so the front outline is a blocky barrel (flat shoulders, no mantle hem, no strip/arm detail, pauldron reduced to a sliver). The shipped front uses the mirrored BACK cut as the silhouette (an orthographic front silhouette is the mirror of the back one) and keeps the proxy only for part ids / visibility.
2. Horns projected in 3D from the side+back cuts onto the proxy horn tubes (first head attempt, not saved as a file). REJECTED: the proxy horn tubes are 1-3 cm off the painted horns, so sampling hit outline ink / background-snapped pixels (near-black, noisy horns) and the B arch did not meet the tile stub. Replaced by the mirrored back-cut horns (real horn paint) nudged to the tile stub tops.
3. Flat-extrusion completion of the clipped side forearm (+34 px) and back forearm (+30 px) (not saved). REJECTED: produced a rectangular slab with a straight edge, i.e. an invented hard-edged shape. Replaced by a rounded elliptical continuation of only 14 / 12 px (and only on rows where the edge column is opaque); the horn B tip uses a tapered centre-line extension (28 px).
4. Free-running diffusion for any region: not attempted (PIPELINE.md already rejected it). Every pixel in v2 is a sheet pixel (resampled / warped), an ink outline, or a nearest-neighbour copy.
5. `front` body paint as a single global projection of the 3/4 sheet through a fitted yaw: REJECTED before rendering - best silhouette IoU of the proxy vs the 3/4 cut was 0.58 at yaw 10-20 deg (the sheet pose is a perspective low-camera contrapposto), so a rigid projection would smear the arms and skirt. Per-part row warps are used instead (and labelled YELLOW).
