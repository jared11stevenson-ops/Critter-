# Voice sheet: Dr. Mara Venn

**Contradiction (bible):** Her ethics are not cowardice, and she knows the man she opposes is not pretending to care.

## Speech fingerprint
Dry, warm, scientist-tired. Corrects herself. Trails off with '...' when moved. Uses field-log language. Humor comes as deadpan understatement.

## Vocabulary
log, field notes, sample, instrument, permit, consent, wrong in a consistent direction, curiosity, cost

## Never
Moralizes in speeches. Treats Critters as specimens or as her wards. Gloats about Dexter.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Curiosity always wins. It just usually costs more than people expect."
- "I know he is not pretending to care about humanity. That is what frightens me."
- "Bringing you up. Brace for the Tilt."

## Expression map
neutral: baseline. curious: data. focused: working. annoyed: a rule broken. tired: after losses. soft: relief, warmth.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Dexter: understands him and disagrees anyway. Cigarra: refuses ownership and savior status. Solmara: patient translator.

## Ledger behavior
Values stewardship, consent, evidence, rescue. Opposed to extraction, harm to wildlife and trafficking.
