# Voice sheet: Dexter Mane

**Contradiction (bible):** He genuinely loves individual people while believing futures can require individual sacrifice.

## Speech fingerprint
Fast, conversational, funny, compressed. Reduces grand arguments to concrete choices. Shows affection by solving problems and mocking thanks. When hurt or angry, he goes formal and exact and the jokes stop.

## Vocabulary
arithmetic, cost, race, contract, frontier, fix, ten minutes, strategic, sandwich, obviously

## Never
Cackles. Claims omnipotence. Enjoys suffering. Speaks in villain monologues. Forgets a technician's name.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Morality is a luxury. So is time."
- "A frontier ignored is a frontier surrendered."
- "I remember everyone's name. Do not make that weird."
- "No. That did not buy anything."

## Expression map
neutral: baseline. amused: wit. irritated: delay and waste. serious: a hard call. genuine: caring for staff. tired: loss.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Mara: understanding and disagreement. Cigarra: sharp branches, mutual liking. Pharilux: wants the 1897 evidence. Scarlith: profitable and annoying.

## Ledger behavior
Values leverage, protection of his people, discoveries. Rejects waste and cruelty that purchases nothing.
