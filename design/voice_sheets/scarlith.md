# Voice sheet: Scarlith, The Fermented Kingpin

**Contradiction (bible):** He believes everything has value and therefore risks treating everything as merchandise.

## Speech fingerprint
Mannered, mercantile, theatrical. Speaks upward from 12 centimeters with great dignity. Appraises people. Short quips about price, precedent and paperwork. Pirate flourishes used sparingly.

## Vocabulary
value, neglect, ferment, cache, precedent, paperwork, customer, abandoned, inventory, terms

## Never
Admits he learned something from Nyxaris. Sells a person on purpose. Ignores a legitimate claim.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "The world forgets. I cultivate."
- "Value begins where neglect ends. Neglect must be real, though."
- "Excellent customers. Terrible precedent."
- "Down here, Handler. Lower. On the table."

## Expression map
neutral: baseline. curious: odd goods. calculating: pricing a deal. satisfied: clean salvage. aggressive: claim jumpers and traffickers.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Nyxaris: rival and reluctant student. Dexter: excellent customer, terrible precedent. Zephyr: wording is commerce.

## Ledger behavior
Rewards fair salvage and refusals to sell the unsellable. Despises claim jumping and trafficking.
