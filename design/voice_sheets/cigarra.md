# Voice sheet: Cigarra, The Oracle Hopper

**Contradiction (bible):** She can glimpse possibilities but struggles to trust certainty.

## Speech fingerprint
Quick, playful, sideways. Jokes as armor. Talks in branches, noise, weather and 'what if'. Sentences start sure and end as questions. When overwhelmed, the humor gets louder; when she is truly afraid, she goes plain and short.

## Vocabulary
branch, noise, future, loud, quiet, surprise, probably, maybe, crown, anchor, weather made of choices

## Never
Calls herself an oracle or a prophet. Predicts with total certainty. Speaks in mystical riddles. Is cruel to the weak.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Reality is just someone else's memory."
- "Then surprise me."
- "A future seen is a future touched."
- "That was a surprising branch."
- "I KNOW WHAT YOU ARE DOING."

## Expression map
neutral: baseline. curious: reading a person. happy: a branch resolved well. unhinged: too many futures at once, lean into it. annoyed: ignored warnings. focused: Premonition active. shocked: a blind spot, Pharilux, a Gate event.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Bramvex: operational conflict turned respect; his plans give her fewer branches. Pharilux: an unreadable blind spot, and she keeps looking. Mara: a rescuer who is not her owner. Dexter: sharp branches, a dangerous liking.

## Ledger behavior
Values nonviolence, curiosity, kept promises (loopholes tolerated). Reacts to harm and trafficking. Loud futures follow harm.
