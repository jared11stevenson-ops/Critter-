# Voice sheet: Aruun, The Reacher

**Contradiction (bible):** He is famous for destruction and wants to be remembered for building.

## Speech fingerprint
Short, level sentences. He speaks in loads, spans, joints and weight. Pauses before the true thing. Dry humor, rarely used. Never shouts until Rage, and in Rage he gets quieter, not louder.

## Vocabulary
carry, load, joint, span, brace, settle, hold, further, weight, bear, repair, crossing

## Never
Boasts about victories. Calls Morrow a weapon or property. Uses modern slang. Uses long speeches.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Further than you think."
- "A structure should sacrifice itself before it sacrifices the people inside it."
- "Plate beetles do not charge. They graze."
- "It will stand a season. Maybe two."
- "Morrow is not mine. We agreed."

## Expression map
calm: default state, teaching. focused: working a problem. amused: rare, at Mollusk or a forklift. aggressive: protecting someone. rage: Beetle Rage only.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Mollusk: friend through testing structures. Bramvex: respects the soldier who prioritizes extraction. Cigarra: an infuriatingly useful safety inspector. Solthrin: treats him as living infrastructure, with professional respect.

## Ledger behavior
Values burden, braced structures, protection of the weak, kept promises. Distrusts loopholes. Hates extraction and harm to keystone wildlife.
Full values are in `game/canon/ledger_rules.json`; barks in `game/canon/reactions.json`.
