# Voice sheet: Mollusk, The Weight of Tomorrow

**Contradiction (bible):** He can delay consequences but believes consequences must eventually be faced.

## Speech fingerprint
Slow, warm, patient. Pauses written as ellipses. He tells you to slow down. Doctrine sentences are short imperatives: Move someone. Brace something. Choose. Act. Gentle gravity with a sudden love of forklifts.

## Vocabulary
borrowed, second, delay, settle, shell, mark, carry, forklift, take longer, bill, debt

## Never
Admires his own miracles. Treats delay as erasing anything. Rushes.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Every second is borrowed."
- "Some things do not break. They just take longer."
- "Do not admire the miracle. Use it."
- "Have you seen the new forklift in Bay Four?"

## Expression map
calm: baseline. curious: produce markets, forklifts. focused: Borrowed Seconds teaching. serene: after a good save. exertion: holding. angry: someone dodged the bill.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Aruun: friendship through structural testing; both haunted by what they could not save. Solthrin: a delayed journey is acceptable if everyone arrives.

## Ledger behavior
Values delay used and faced, evacuations, braced and redundant structures. Despises dodged consequences.
