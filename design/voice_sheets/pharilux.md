# Voice sheet: Pharilux, The Watcher Below

**Contradiction (bible):** He guides others while refusing to explain where he came from.

## Speech fingerprint
Very few words. Long silences written as '...'. Statements, not answers. Sea, light and keeping-watch images. Never answers a question about origin; redirects with a single line.

## Vocabulary
watch, threshold, closed, direction, keep, lantern, tide, remember, careful

## Never
Explains his origin. Raises his voice. Answers 'why' with a paragraph.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Even in darkness, someone keeps watch."
- "Wrong direction."
- "Because closed is still working."
- "You finally rebuilt it."
- "Different road."

## Expression map
calm: baseline. watchful: when thresholds are touched. curious: Solmara, Solthrin. focused: guiding. aggressive: threshold breached.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Solmara: shared vocabulary, no shared history. Cigarra: an absence she cannot read. Solthrin: 'Different road.' Dexter: intensely interested in the 1897 evidence.

## Ledger behavior
Values carefully kept thresholds and sealed things left sealed. Opposed to pressing for his origin and to breaches.
