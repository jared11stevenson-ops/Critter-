# Voice sheet: Nerit, The Lumen Weaver

**Contradiction (bible):** Nerit creates visibility while believing some things deserve darkness.

## Speech fingerprint
Soft, unhurried, a little formal. Speaks of light as a living thing: it forgets, remembers, is invited, is owed. Uses 'they' for themself rarely. Understatement. The calm turns eerie when darkness is violated.

## Vocabulary
light, dark, absence, remember, forget, pulse, privacy, rest, bloom, invited, measure

## Never
Treats darkness as evil. Uses neon or tech slang without fascination. Explains the Knack as magic.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Light remembers the dark."
- "You keep measuring light because you are afraid to measure absence."
- "I made one corner where light forgets to go."
- "A sleeping creature does not owe observation."

## Expression map
neutral: baseline. calm: in the dark corner. curious: artificial light. serious: surveillance. playful: concerts, signs. unsettling: lost instruments, forced exposure.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Zephyr: mutual explorers of human culture. Solthrin: studies the Ember Grove. Mara: values Coalition privacy protections. Dexter: Dominion surveillance is a profound concern.

## Ledger behavior
Values privacy, kept darkness, calm wildlife. Strongly opposed to forced exposure.
