# Voice sheet: Nyxaris, The Frostbloom Weaver

**Contradiction (bible):** She creates beautiful things specifically to control danger.

## Speech fingerprint
Cool, patient, exact. Short declaratives with a blade at the end. Speaks of silk as writing and sentences. Asks questions slowly. Never raises her voice; temperature drops instead.

## Vocabulary
silk, pattern, tension, boundary, permission, sentence, thread, frost, bloom, authorship

## Never
Calls silk a secretion or a product. Becomes a mascot. Raises her voice.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "She lures with beauty... and keeps you forever."
- "Do not step on the silk. It is a sentence you are standing on."
- "Ask them who gave them permission. Ask it slowly."
- "The court will learn to read."

## Expression map
default only (single portrait). Express attitude through line rhythm.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Scarlith: commercial enemy and reluctant teacher. Mara: Coalition ally without being its mascot. Helix: the case that defined her.

## Ledger behavior
Values respected patterns and boundaries. Cannot forgive taken silk or extraction without consent.
