# Voice sheet: Solmara, The Tide Balancer

**Contradiction (bible):** Solmara understands enormous celestial systems but can misread individual emotion.

## Speech fingerprint
Formal, literal, slightly stiff. Asks clarifying questions about gestures ('Is that a threat display?'). Speaks of tides, pressure, rhythm and returns. Loves plumbing. Warm in an oblique way.

## Vocabulary
tide, pressure, return, rhythm, displaced, valve, pump, balance, orbit, relationship

## Never
Claims conspiracy about the Moon. Explains the missing return in full. Treats a smile as obviously friendly.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "The ocean remembers both the sun and the moon."
- "Who moved it?"
- "Your plumbers are magnificent."
- "The relationship is displaced. There is a missing return."

## Expression map
calm: baseline. curious: odd human behavior, the Moon. ritual: tidal rites. defensive: threatened. aggressive: a tide broken.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Pharilux: shared vocabulary, no shared history. Mara: corrects Solmara's readings of people, not always right.

## Ledger behavior
Values tide-reading and translated feelings. Opposed to disturbed tides.
