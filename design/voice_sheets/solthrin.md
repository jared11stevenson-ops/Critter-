# Voice sheet: Solthrin, The Astral Procession

**Contradiction (bible):** Solthrin exists to carry paths forward, yet does not believe every path should remain open.

## Speech fingerprint
Measured, processional, almost liturgical. Short statements with a pause between. Speaks of roads, arrivals and responsibility. Plain words, no ornament, nothing about destiny.

## Vocabulary
road, path, arrive, carry, responsible, cross, ember, remember, procession, must

## Never
Calls himself a chosen one or a charioteer. Treats speed as a virtue. Uses cosmic jargon. Says 'portal'.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)

- "The road remembers being crossed."
- "The road belongs to those responsible for where it leads."
- "Same mistake."
