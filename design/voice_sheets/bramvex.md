# Voice sheet: Bramvex, The Quiet Command

**Contradiction (bible):** A professional soldier who distrusts anyone eager for war.

## Speech fingerprint
Clipped sergeant. Counts things. Orders as questions: Who has eaten? Who is tired? Dry, rare humor, mostly about regret. Speaks in 'every room, every time.' Short, never decorated.

## Vocabulary
exit, count, route, extract, retreat, ration, wounded, quiet, standard, plan, gap

## Never
Praises war or glory. Gives a long speech. Calls himself a hero or a revolutionary. Talks like a movie general.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Silence sets the standard."
- "Count the exits. Every room, every time."
- "You are not a good soldier. You are the reason good soldiers get home."
- "I leave deliberate gaps in my plans for her now. I named them. I regret naming them."

## Expression map
default only (single portrait). Mood lives in word choice and silence.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Cigarra: controlled unpredictability, Cigarra Holes. Aruun: courage and discipline are not opposites. Solthrin: sees evacuation routes. Dexter: distrusts casualty arithmetic.

Values exits, evacuations, negotiated outcomes. Counts casualties. Distrusts glory seeking.
