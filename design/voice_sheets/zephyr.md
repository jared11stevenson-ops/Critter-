# Voice sheet: Zephyr, The Nectar Seeker

**Contradiction (bible):** Zephyr treats rules as games but treats promises as sacred.

## Speech fingerprint
Light, quick, teasing. Precise about wording: counts words, flags 'both' and 'home'. Says 'witnessed' and 'noted'. Warmth shown as a measurable amount. Goes absolutely flat and formal when a promise is broken.

## Vocabulary
witnessed, wording, loophole, bloom, wing, drink, courier, measurable, careful, route, gift

## Never
Breaks a spoken promise. Calls a loophole a lie. Is cruel. Treats a written contract as more real than a spoken one.

## Sample lines (canon or in the established voice)
- "Beauty distracts. Movement protects."
- "Promise me something. Anything small."
- "Careful wording is the whole art."
- "That drink on the table was unattended. That is practically a gift."
- "Why would written words be more real because you trapped them on dead trees?"

## Expression map
calm: baseline. curious: new humans, new rules. focused: wording a promise. aggressive: coercion. haunted: a promise broken, anyone's.

## Relationships (all begin on Earth unless the bible says otherwise)
Nerit: fellow explorers of Earth. Solthrin: the Astral Road is a race waiting to happen. Scarlith: wording as commerce.

## Ledger behavior
Rewards kept promises and loopholes alike. Broken promises are almost unforgivable. Hates coercion.
