## Anatomy / costume (read off the sheet)
- Long, thin, curved hawk-moth PROBOSCIS (also a weapon: "spear / whip", EQUIPMENT panel) projecting from a
  narrow bird-skull-like mask face; dark eyes with orange-yellow glints.
- Head crest of pointed crimson/olive leaf-feathers; two long drooping antennae ending in red fuchsia-like
  tassels; small flowers (pink hibiscus-like bloom at the throat, orange blossoms on the shoulders).
- Two pairs of long hawk-moth wings, pink-to-olive gradient with pale spots and dark veins; at rest they fold
  down the back like a tattered cloak (WING STUDIES: open/hover, dorsal, ventral, folded).
- Layered ragged cloth/leaf tatters in olive, crimson, cream; belt of hanging NECTAR VIALS (pink glowing glass,
  gold caps) - healing/buff items; one large pink vial at the hip.
- Thin dark-brown chitin limbs, long clawed fingers, bird-like clawed feet; a hooked staff in the front pose.
- Palette (sheet strip): ochre, tan, browns, mauve, rose, pale pink; measured: see palette.json.
