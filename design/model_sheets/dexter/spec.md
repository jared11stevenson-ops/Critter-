# Dexter Mane - modelling / art spec
Source: tools/source_art/dexter_sheet.jpg (1400 px), lore bible v12, canon.json.
Hi-res views: hires/front_hd.png (hero 3/4 pose), hires/back_hd.png (BACK VIEW / SILHOUETTE panel, coat with
red lining). Boards: turnaround.png (185 cm bar), palette.json/png, expressions.png (6 in-game portraits).
**There is no side view on the sheet**: in game, side.png == front.png (3/4, faces right). A true side/front
orthographic view must be requested from the creator before any 3D work.

## Identity (sheet text)
Director / Scientist / Strategist - "The Ends Justify the Means". Age 37. **Height 6'1" (185 cm)** - canon.json
1.85 m (match). Build lean/strong. Leader of the Human Dominion; founding scientist of the Gate Project.
Specialty: Gate Technology, Weaponization, Knack Extraction, Black Ops. Quote: "Morality is a luxury. So is time."

## Field outfit (hero art = the game look)
- Hair: dark, tousled, swept back, with a **grey/white streak** on the right side of the parting; stubble beard.
- Eyes grey-green ("tired but sharp"); small scar above the RIGHT brow; warm tan skin. Cigarette almost always.
- Long black field coat, collar up, sleeves pushed to the forearm, **Dominion red sigil on the left shoulder**,
  **red coat lining** (very visible from the back: the whole lining panel is red with the black/red sigil).
- Black shirt, ID lanyard/badge on a red cord, black utility belt with pouches, thigh holster with the custom
  sidearm on the right thigh, black cargo trousers, black lace-up combat boots with red sigil detail.
- Fingerless/half gloves with a wrist strap; ring on the right hand; utility knife "almost always on him".
- Tattoo: faction symbol on the left ribs (not visible in field outfit).

## Other outfits on the sheet
- Lab / Field: white lab coat over black, goggles on the forehead (second hero figure; it overlaps the main
  figure on the sheet, which is why the billboard's right coat edge is a straight cut).
- Casual: jacket, slumped in a chair, smoking.

## Faction visual language
Human Dominion: black + blood red, angular trident/sword sigil (see the FACTION panel, red on black).

## Palette (measured, palette.json)
Coat/trousers #231f1f / #2e2927 / #0d0c0c; skin #946751; lining red #6e302a (back) / #5b322d; highlights #d3bda7.

## Known limitations
Hero pose only (3/4, hand in pocket, weight on one leg). The back view comes from a small panel (~230 px tall
on the sheet) - the x4 version is upscaler-reconstructed detail. The right coat edge of the hero is hidden by
the overlapping lab-coat figure and is cut straight.
