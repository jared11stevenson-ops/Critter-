# Cigarra - 3D modelling spec (from tools/source_art/cigarra_sheet.jpg via hires/ x4 views)
Height 165 cm (sheet). Psychic witch, Brazilian treehopper inspiration. All features below are read off the sheet.
- Head: pale/white shaggy hair, pointed ears, golden-yellow eyes with dark liner, dark third-eye spot on forehead,
  black face markings. CROWN: dark branching stalk from the back of the head with ~7 glossy black spheres
  (treehopper "Bocydium" crown); retracted/expanded states on sheet (crown_x4.png).
- Torso: cream/olive cropped top with dark trim, bare midriff, dark hooded jacket (deep violet-black) with olive
  moss edging, sleeves rolled, bandaged wrists/forearms.
- Wing cloak: two pairs of translucent lime-green wing panels with dark veins and black spots, hanging from the
  shoulders/back; folded in back view, spread in front view; hood with gold sigil tab on the back.
- Belt: brown leather with buckle, hanging charms (pale skull-like gourds, egg pod), orange cords.
- Trousers: baggy violet-black harem pants, cuffed at the shin; bandage-wrapped shins; dark leaf-trimmed boots.
- Palette: palette.json (k-means per view). Silhouette: big crown spheres + wide wing cloak + baggy pants.
Budget target: <= 25k tris, one 2048 texture set, rig compatible with CharacterModel (same API as Aruun).
