## Canon
Homeland Thornmarch. Knack: Camouflage / Sensory Adaptation / Tactical Terrain Use. Weapon "Diplomacy".
Sheet: Class Vanguard/Initiator, Role Disruptor/Zone Control, Inspiration Prickly Stick Insect / Spiked Bat /
Sergeant; Tone Disciplined/Camouflaged/Brutal. Bible: "approved prickly-stick-insect design, ragged membranous
wings, field equipment, restrained red insignia and thorned weapon." Quote: "Silence sets the standard."
**Height: not printed on the sheet.** canon.json 2.3 m is consistent with the SCALE COMPARISON panel (human
silhouette ~ 0.75x his height). Flag for creator confirmation.

## Anatomy / costume (read off the sheet)
- Very thin, long-limbed stick-insect body in bark browns with thorn spurs on every limb segment; long thin
  antennae (red-tipped) rising from the head; mantis-like narrow face with a red eye (head studies).
- Military peaked cap (olive/dark, red band) worn low; olive field jacket, crossed chest straps, ammunition/pouch
  belt with many brown leather pouches.
- Bat-like ragged membrane wings (tan/olive, with holes), folded like a cloak from the shoulders; long tattered
  dark-red banner/tabard with gold chevron sergeant insignia (front left and back).
- Lantern (red-orange glass) hanging from a hooked pole/branch held over the shoulder; small lantern on the hip.
- Weapon "Diplomacy": long thorned club/branch with a spiked head (WEAPON & GEAR DETAILS panel shows 6 variants).
- Palette: bark browns, olive, muted khaki, blood-red cloth, gold insignia (palette.json; sheet palette strip:
  ochre, tan, crimson, dark red, brown, olive grey, khaki, pale beige).

## Silhouette / notes
Tall and stooped, angular; the wings + banner give a ragged cloak outline; antennae and lantern pole break the
top of the silhouette. Animation panel: idle (blend in), walk, stalk low, sprint, wing flare, vault, wall cling,
ambush drop, thorn guard. The side view's pale area between the legs is translucent wing membrane on the sheet
(kept, not background).
