## Canon
Homeland The Velvet Warrens. Knack Fermentation / Decomposition / Cultivation. Sheet: Class Disruptor/Controller,
Role Zone Control/Resource Denial, Inspiration Red Velvet Mite + Pirate + Cheese (aging/decay/value);
Tone Deceptive/Opportunistic/Regal. **HEIGHT 12 cm (4.7")** - canon.json 0.12 m (match).
NOTE: the sheet's scale figure is a human silhouette drawn SHORTER than Scarlith next to "12 cm"; read as a
"scale-state" comparison, not literal. In game he stands on the Common Table at 12 cm (dialogue: "Down here,
Handler. Lower. On the table.") - consistent with canon.

  shell, dripping cream tatters; barrel/"treasure cache" on the back (back view).

- Palette: crimson #8c3b2b/#572520, near-black #0e0707, cheese #edba7a/#b77743 (palette.json; sheet swatches
