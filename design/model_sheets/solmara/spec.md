## Anatomy (read off the sheet)
- True pycnogonid (sea spider) layout: tiny central body, 8 extremely long jointed walking legs (pale ivory/bone
  plates with gold edging and deep navy-blue segments), leg tips pointed; legs arch high above the body.
- Head/proboscis: long beak-like proboscis in ivory and gold; dark compound "eyes".
- SUN/MOON CORE: a glowing gold sun disc (energy/push) with radiating gold spikes paired with a black-and-white
  crescent MOON CORE (gravity/pull) mounted above/behind the head - the eclipse motif. Gold rays form a halo.
- Translucent tide-silk webbing between legs (TIDE SILK STRUCTURE panel); pressure sensors on legs.
- Palette: ivory #eccfa7, gold #de9b50, deep navy #363e51 / #171518, rust #7f4722 (palette.json).
