# Solmara - style guide

Everything here is read off the original sheet art (see ortho/ and head/); `[measured]` = computed by tools/refsheets, the rest is the artist's reading and is the checklist for matching the look in 3D.

## Line weight
- [measured] outline stroke is about 2.9 px on a 1500-px-tall figure = 0.20% of height = 11 mm at canon height.
- Fine dark-navy ink, thin; delicate over many tiny spikes.

## Colour blocking
- [measured] dominant colours (share of opaque pixels): #1d191b 19%, #e0a45f 14%, #262b38 13%, #0a0a0b 12%, #3f4a61 9%, #492e21 9%
- Gold + ivory + deep navy on parchment (turnaround) / dark ocean backgrounds; gold glows.

## Shading style
- Painterly glow around the sun core; legs have cel rim light in gold.
- For 3D: use a toon/stepped ramp or very low-frequency baked AO; do NOT bake photographic soft shadows into albedo.

## Shape language / silhouette
- Huge spindly arch of 8 long legs over a tiny body; radial spikes around the cores; vertical symmetric in front/back.

## Design locks (must never change)
- True pycnogonid (sea spider) layout: tiny body, 8 very long jointed legs, long beak-like proboscis
- Gold sun disc with radiating spikes paired with black/white crescent moon core
- Ivory/gold plates with navy segments
- 5.5 m tall (scale-state)

## Views and authority
- Proportions and heights: side/back views (orthographic where marked in metadata.json). Costume/colour: all views. 3/4 'front' views are NOT orthographic - never snap to them for widths without checking the side/back.
