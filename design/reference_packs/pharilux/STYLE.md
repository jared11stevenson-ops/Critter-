# Pharilux - style guide

Everything here is read off the original sheet art (see ortho/ and head/); `[measured]` = computed by tools/refsheets, the rest is the artist's reading and is the checklist for matching the look in 3D.

## Line weight
- [measured] outline stroke is about 3.2 px on a 1500-px-tall figure = 0.21% of height = 6 mm at canon height.
- Dark brown-black ink, medium; fine orange chain lines drawn as thin coloured line, not ink.

## Colour blocking
- [measured] dominant colours (share of opaque pixels): #332c28 17%, #1f1b19 16%, #e5cfae 12%, #0c0b0a 12%, #b4a287 12%, #4b4842 10%
- Charcoal blue-grey and brown with orange/amber glow accents; pale cream wings.

## Shading style
- Cel with strong warm glow from the lantern windows (painted, hard edged); metal highlights in orange.
- For 3D: use a toon/stepped ramp or very low-frequency baked AO; do NOT bake photographic soft shadows into albedo.

## Shape language / silhouette
- Tall, hunched, top-heavy: a tower-like lantern rises over the shoulder like a lighthouse; ragged wings and cloak hang to the ground; long antennae arc forward.

## Design locks (must never change)
- Lighthouse lantern with glowing windows carried on the back/shoulder
- Moth-like head with long antennae and glowing amber eyes
- Tattered pale wings
- Dark cloak with orange chain/anchor/sigil ornament
- Hanging pendant lantern
- 260 cm

## Views and authority
- Proportions and heights: side/back views (orthographic where marked in metadata.json). Costume/colour: all views. 3/4 'front' views are NOT orthographic - never snap to them for widths without checking the side/back.
