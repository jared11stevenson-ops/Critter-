# Nyxaris - style guide

Everything here is read off the original sheet art (see ortho/ and head/); `[measured]` = computed by tools/refsheets, the rest is the artist's reading and is the checklist for matching the look in 3D.

## Line weight
- [measured] outline stroke is about 3.3 px on a 1500-px-tall figure = 0.22% of height = 4 mm at canon height.
- Dark plum-black ink, thin and wiry on petals, heavier on the body.

## Colour blocking
- [measured] dominant colours (share of opaque pixels): #f0d4c2 21%, #1f1925 20%, #383345 13%, #c8b3b4 11%, #a48f94 9%, #ed94a7 6%
- High-contrast: black body against pink, pale blue and white petals; orange/yellow micro-accents.

## Shading style
- Cel with translucent gradients on petals; fur painted in flat white-grey shards.
- For 3D: use a toon/stepped ramp or very low-frequency baked AO; do NOT bake photographic soft shadows into albedo.

## Shape language / silhouette
- Wide, hunched, heavy: a spiky flower-like crest over a broad four-pointed stance with long drooping antennae; very ragged outline.

## Design locks (must never change)
- Black chitin body with pink/blue/white frost-petal crest and mantle
- Two long antennae ending in hanging fuchsia-like buds
- White-grey fur ruff
- Multi-legged, pointed black legs with blue tips
- 200 cm

## Views and authority
- Proportions and heights: side/back views (orthographic where marked in metadata.json). Costume/colour: all views. 3/4 'front' views are NOT orthographic - never snap to them for widths without checking the side/back.
