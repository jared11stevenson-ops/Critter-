# Bramvex - style guide

Everything here is read off the original sheet art (see ortho/ and head/); `[measured]` = computed by tools/refsheets, the rest is the artist's reading and is the checklist for matching the look in 3D.

## Line weight
- [measured] outline stroke is about 3.9 px on a 1500-px-tall figure = 0.26% of height = 6 mm at canon height.
- Heavy irregular black ink, thorn-spiky; broken lines on cloth.

## Colour blocking
- [measured] dominant colours (share of opaque pixels): #201a15 19%, #0c0b09 18%, #372820 17%, #602d23 10%, #4d3d2f 9%, #754f35 8%
- Bark browns, olive greens, tan membranes, ONE restrained red (cloak, cap band, eye, lantern glow).

## Shading style
- Flat cel with dark browns for depth; gold chevron emblem painted.
- For 3D: use a toon/stepped ramp or very low-frequency baked AO; do NOT bake photographic soft shadows into albedo.

## Shape language / silhouette
- Very tall and thin; spindly long limbs with thorns, huge bat wings and banner behind; hat and antennae at top.

## Design locks (must never change)
- Prickly stick-insect body with thorns on every limb
- Peaked cap with red band, red eye, long red-tipped antennae
- Bat-like ragged wings
- Crimson banner cloak with gold chevron emblem
- Lantern + thorned weapon
- Height 2.3 m unconfirmed

## Views and authority
- Proportions and heights: side/back views (orthographic where marked in metadata.json). Costume/colour: all views. 3/4 'front' views are NOT orthographic - never snap to them for widths without checking the side/back.
