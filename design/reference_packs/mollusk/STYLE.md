# Mollusk - style guide

Everything here is read off the original sheet art (see ortho/ and head/); `[measured]` = computed by tools/refsheets, the rest is the artist's reading and is the checklist for matching the look in 3D.

## Line weight
- [measured] outline stroke is about 2.6 px on a 1500-px-tall figure = 0.17% of height = 4 mm at canon height.
- Heavy irregular dark-brown ink; line breaks in moss and tatters.

## Colour blocking
- [measured] dominant colours (share of opaque pixels): #322420 17%, #120f0e 15%, #4b3a33 14%, #d6a05e 13%, #765935 9%, #6c3521 8%
- Earth tones: tan skin, brown/amber shell, olive foliage, red/orange accents, deep purple cloth.

## Shading style
- Cel with warm shadow; shell has painted ring bands and glossy highlights.
- For 3D: use a toon/stepped ramp or very low-frequency baked AO; do NOT bake photographic soft shadows into albedo.

## Shape language / silhouette
- Tall upright slug body with a huge round shell on the back; eye stalks as the topmost shape; ragged garments spread the silhouette; heavy feet.

## Design locks (must never change)
- Two long eye stalks with round eyes
- Large spiral shell on the back with plants/flowers
- Layered tattered garments with moss
- Slug skin (no humanoid hair)
- 220 cm

## Views and authority
- Proportions and heights: side/back views (orthographic where marked in metadata.json). Costume/colour: all views. 3/4 'front' views are NOT orthographic - never snap to them for widths without checking the side/back.
